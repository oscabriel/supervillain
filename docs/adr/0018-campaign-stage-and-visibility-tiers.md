# The campaign stage: cards shown in tiers, outside undo, in a mode that follows the fight

## Decision

`TableState.stage` is a top-level slice beside `core`, outside the undo snapshot. It holds `cards` (each a player-safe `StageCard` with its `tier`, `revealed` or `on-table`, and the `since` seq), the current `scene`, `trackers` (clocks, counters and quests, each `gm-only` or `revealed`), the `recap` (its latest 50 lines) and an optional `override` of the mode. Content has three tiers: **gm-only** is everything not in `stage.cards`, **revealed** is in the players' codex, **on-table** is also shown on the stage. The GM moves cards with logged commands, each refused to anyone else: `/show <card>` (`core.stage-pinned`; a scene becomes the scene and the previous one drops to revealed, others pin, at most 6), `/unpin` (`core.stage-unpinned`, back to revealed), `/reveal` (`core.card-revealed`), `/hide` (`core.card-hidden`, which deletes the card from the state outright), `/tracker` and `/tick` (`core.tracker-set`, `core.tracker-removed`; `/tracker <id> clock|counter|quest [max] [=start] [--] <label>`, where `--` lets a label start with a digit, which the prep panel's Track line always writes with the file's `max` and `value`; a new tracker is gm-only until `/tracker <id> show`, and a gm-only tracker's changes write no feed line), `/recap` (`core.recap-added`, refused in `decide` when it cleans to nothing) and `/sync [card]` (`core.content-synced`, the shown cards read again, tiers kept; only the cards that changed ride in the event, and `Nothing changed.` is refused). `/show`, `/reveal` and `/sync` need the card: `intake` asks the host through a `HostNeed { kind: "card" | "cards" }` answered from the campaign's files, so the card rides in the event and replay never reads a file. `cardProblem` checks every card in `decide` and again in `apply`: its key (`<kind>:<id>`), a title and subtitle of at most 80 characters, at most 8 KB of body and 4 KB of read-aloud, art of at most 40 lines of 80 columns, a map of at most 60 columns by 40 rows, and no terminal control in any text; the codex's 200 cards are checked in both too. The stage's mode is derived: `stageModeOf` is `encounter` while any ruleset is in combat (`ds.combat.active`, `sw.fight`, `pf.combat`, `ff.order`) and `campaign` otherwise; `/stage campaign|encounter` records an override with the combat state it was made in, and every fold drops it once combat changes, so it holds only until the next fight starts or ends (`/stage auto` drops it at once). `stageOf(state, actor, ruleset)` projects plain objects for the seat: the on-table scene and pinned cards, the codex of revealed and on-table entries, the trackers (only revealed ones for a player), the recap and the ruleset's party. `/codex [card]` reads the codex at a seat; its Tab offers only keys in `stage.cards`, and only the GM's `/codex` adds the file's GM notes, read live and never logged.

## Why

- Showing the table a handout is a fact of play, so it belongs in the log; what the GM merely prepared does not.
- Undoing a fight's start should not take back a clue the GM already showed; the stage, like membership, is not a move in the game.
- A hidden card that lingered in the state would reach a player through any future projection; deleting it closes that door.
- The GM rarely wants to flip the stage by hand at every fight, but sometimes wants to hold it for a beat; an override that lapses at the next transition does both without a stale setting.

## What breaks if you change this

- Keeping `gm-only` cards in `stage.cards` with a flag makes every projection, completion and feed line responsible for filtering them.
- Folding `stage` inside the undo snapshot lets `/undo` of an unrelated roll un-show a card, or a redo re-show one the GM has since hidden.
- Building the card in `decide` from files breaks replay and lets the log disagree with what players saw.
- Dropping the `apply`-side checks lets a hand-edited log put terminal escapes on every player's screen.
