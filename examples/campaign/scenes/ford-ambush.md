The road dips toward the ford between a ruined wall and a stand of brush. Fresh cart ruts end at the water, and the reeds on the far bank have been trampled flat.

Old Mara, the ferry-keeper, waves from her hut on the near bank, then thinks better of it.

## Read Aloud

The Ford Road runs down to the water and stops. Across the shallows, someone has dragged a cart sideways over the far bank, and behind it, a dozen faces watch you come.

A voice carries over the river: "Toll's gone up."

## GM Notes

The chief stays behind the cart until a hero crosses the ford. Tick the alarm each round the slingers aren't engaged; at 4, two more bruisers come up the road from the east.

Mara has been paid to look the other way. She tells the truth if a hero offers to cover her debt.
