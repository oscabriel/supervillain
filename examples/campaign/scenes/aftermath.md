The water runs red and then clear again. The cart on the far bank holds a month of other people's goods, sorted and tagged as if for sale.

## Read Aloud

The last of the bandits splashes off downstream. On the far bank, the cart creaks as it settles, and something in it ticks like a clock.

## GM Notes

The ticking is a music box meant for the mayor's daughter. The ledger names the buyer only as "the Lantern".
