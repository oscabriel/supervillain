A wiry woman with a pole longer than she is tall. She has kept the ford for forty years and knows every stone under the water.

## GM Notes

She owes the bandit chief twelve silver and has been signalling the gang when caravans come. If pressed, she fights as a cutpurse and runs at the first wound.
