A sample one-shot for trying supervillain: four level-1 heroes, one road, one ford and a gang that has been taxing travellers at knifepoint for a month.

## GM Notes

Run the ambush, then the aftermath. The chief's ledger points at a bigger story if the table wants one: someone in town has been buying what the bandits take.
