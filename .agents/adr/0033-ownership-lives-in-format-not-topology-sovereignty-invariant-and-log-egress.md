# Ownership lives in format, not topology: the sovereignty invariant and log egress

Resolved in a product-analysis session with oscar, 2026-08-22. The session audited supervillain against Steph Ango's moat taxonomy, asked whether a strictly-hosted model would beat GM-machine hosting, and found the definition carrying a tension it never named: after [ADR-0012](./0012-outbound-only-hybrid-drop-iroh-host-dials-out.md), every remote table already rides company infrastructure — the gateway terminates SSH, shares render folds, brokers all traffic — so "GM-hosted is the center of gravity" had quietly become a claim about where the JSONL rests, not who operates the service. This ADR makes that separation deliberate instead of letting it stay silent drift.

**Decision: ownership lives in format, not topology.** The pillar **"you own your campaign"** is a claim about format and egress: plain JSONL, portable by copy, replayable by anything that speaks the envelope, and able to outlive supervillain entirely ([ADR-0010](./0010-fold-posture-writers-halt-readers-tolerate.md)'s fold posture is what makes that last clause true). It was never load-bearing on which machine runs the Host. Ticket 007's resolution ("GM-hosted rooms as the center of gravity") survives as the **v0 posture** — the create-command-is-the-host shape ships as pinned — but it stops being the content of the ownership pillar, and no future hosting posture can weaken the pillar, because the pillar never depended on it. Rejected: re-reading ticket 007 as licensing gateway-first development; nothing here builds hosted authority or moves v0's posture.

**Decision: the sovereignty invariant — supervillain.app is never load-bearing.** Any table runs indefinitely with our servers unreachable. Concretely: every feature must answer "does this work sovereign?", the way [ADR-0028](./0028-the-reach-checklist-every-lifecycle-transition-states-its-access-consequence.md) made lifecycle transitions answer "what access consequence?". The invariant is a review gate and an engineering discipline, not marketing copy. Known documented exception: publishing is hosted-only at v1 because browsers do not do trust-on-first-use ([ADR-0015](./0015-one-binary-two-roles-gateway-role-selector-and-homelab-state.md)) — play stays sovereign there; the web share loop does not, and the docs must keep saying so plainly. New features that cannot meet the invariant need their own ADR naming why.

**Decision: the three postures are a sovereignty ladder, and every rung is exit-safe.** Hosted (zero friction, our servers) → self-hosted gateway (the same MIT binary on the GM's box, players ssh to the GM's own address per ADR-0015) → local/offline (no network at all). Each rung up trades convenience for control; no rung is a trap. What makes no rung a trap is egress, which is the next decision.

**Decision: `supervillain campaign pull` — one-way log egress, never metered.** The verb materializes a room's Log into a Campaign folder: byte-identical Envelope stream, one direction, no merge, no sync. One-way only is what keeps the single-writer rule intact by construction — bidirectional sync would reopen split-brain problems the event-sourced design exists to avoid. It rides the management-noun side (`campaign`, landed by [#28](https://github.com/oscabriel/supervillain/issues/28) and [#46](https://github.com/oscabriel/supervillain/issues/46)) exactly as [ADR-0015](./0015-one-binary-two-roles-gateway-role-selector-and-homelab-state.md) established for `gateway`; the pinned table verb set (`create` / `join` / `publish`) does not widen. Pull is pillar infrastructure: free at every tier, forever, including after any paid tier debuts. Spec'd in [#135](https://github.com/oscabriel/supervillain/issues/135).

**Named direction, not built:** **authority handoff** (#136) — when a Host dies, a seated player's machine resumes the host role from the shared Log, because replay is byte-identical and the Log needs nothing but itself. This dissolves the accepted launch gap with zero new infrastructure and pre-empts the offline-host demand signal that would otherwise open hosted authority. Not decided here whether it ships; #136 records the open questions (GM-seat continuity, trusted-stamper vouching, split-brain fencing, sidecar reconciliation). Every surface now hardening around single-host assumptions — trusted stamper registration, seat continuity, fold checkpoints — should record its interaction with handoff so no cheap door gets welded shut.

**Consequences:** `product-definition.md` gains dated corrections at the ownership pillar, the center-of-gravity bullet, and the business-shape self-hosting paragraph; the pitch hierarchy is restated under Brand voice (player benefit first, ownership second, agent surface headline, self-hosting demonstrated not shouted). `CONTEXT.md` gains **Sovereignty invariant**, **Sovereignty ladder**, and **Log egress**. Issues [#135](https://github.com/oscabriel/supervillain/issues/135) and [#136](https://github.com/oscabriel/supervillain/issues/136) carry the actionable work. Nothing in this ADR changes v0 scope, the RoomStore seam, or the paid-tier boundary — it changes what the pillars promise and adds one verb-shaped guarantee underneath them.

**Revisit triggers:** the invariant bends only via ADR with a named reason (the v1 publishing exception is the template); pull grows incremental re-pull (`--since`) on measured large-log pain; authorization for pull (owner vs. seated identity) is pinned at triage of #135; authority handoff graduates from named direction to decision if the offline-host tripwire fires before #136's questions are answered.
