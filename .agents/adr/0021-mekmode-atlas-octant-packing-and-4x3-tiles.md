# MEKMODE atlas, octant packing, 4×3-cell tiles: ADR-0007's source and geometry amended

> **Scope fence added 2026-08-04 by [ADR-0030](./0030-closed-pane-frames-tab-traversal-side-rails-and-contextual-key-help.md):** 4×3 atlas glyphs do not enter the Table's Pane frames or alter their geometry. Frames stay one-cell light box drawing in both tiers; the Lobby wordmark remains licensed isolated threshold art.

Resolved 2026-07-30, in-session prototype ratification, closing [#95](https://github.com/oscabriel/supervillain/issues/95)'s watch. The gates #95 named all cleared: MEK.txt granted access to the MEKMODE Text/Dings OTFs directly (the public download remains disabled) with an informal free-use okay; extraction was proven reproducible; and a static table mock at the amended geometry was ratified on a real terminal. This amends [ADR-0007](./0007-pixel-glyph-rendering-tier.md)'s atlas source and packing geometry. **Everything else in ADR-0007 stands unchanged**: the tier decision itself, pixel-default with the text-mode toggle, no capability detection, map-surface-plus-ornamental-chrome scope, the data-only atlas package, and "this changes nothing below the renderer."

**Decision: the atlas source is MEKMODE (Text + Dings), replacing MonoMEK.** MEKMODE is pixel-perfect at a declared 8×12 grid — the CFF outlines sit on an exact 100-units-per-pixel lattice, so the bake is a deterministic rasterization (FreeType, 10 ppem, mono render, no hinting), not ADR-0007's ppem/threshold hunt over a "non-pixel-perfect" monotype. The extraction was validated end to end: all 313 Text + 119 Dings glyphs land in the 8×12 frame with zero clipping, and text rendered from the extracted bitmaps matches MEK.txt's published specimen pixel for pixel. Method, source hashes, and font internals are recorded in the local research note (`.agents/research/mekmode-extraction-note.md`); the bake script must carry them into the ADR-0011 provenance header. MonoMEK remains a design touchstone and ADR-0009's self-hosted webfont on the web side — that choice is independent and unmoved.

**Decision: packing is octants — one tile = 4×3 cells, lossless.** Each terminal cell carries a 2×4-pixel block mapped to Unicode 16 octant characters (`BLOCK OCTANT-*` plus the 26 patterns encoded elsewhere: 20 block elements, U+1CEA0/A3/A8/AB, U+1FBE6/E7). An 8×12 glyph is exactly 12 cells with all 96 bits preserved and the native 2:3 aspect intact. This beats both geometries ADR-0007 weighed: quadrants would render MEKMODE at 4×6 cells stretched 1:3, and #95's anticipated aspect-corrected 8×6 rebake would reach 4×3 only by discarding half the vertical resolution. It is also smaller than the MonoMEK plan (12 cells vs 16 per tile): ~45×17 visible tiles at 188×60. Half-block packing (8×6 cells, universal support, same atlas rows) remains licensed for chrome set-pieces where banner scale is the point — the wordmark, threshold art.

**Decision: the Unicode-16 requirement rides the existing no-detection stance.** Octants are Unicode 16 (2024); modern emulators (Ghostty, Kitty) draw them built-in, older ones show tofu. Per ADR-0007's argument, unchanged: no probe, failure mode is legible tofu plus one keystroke to text mode. A braille middle rung (same 4×3 geometry, universal coverage, dotted look) was prototyped and deliberately **not** added — a second ornamental tier without detection is a toggle nobody asked for. It becomes worth reopening only on real tofu reports from seated players.

**Decision: provenance is recorded honestly as informal permission.** MEKMODE is not CC0 and its page states no license. MEK.txt provided the files and a general free okay (attested in-session 2026-07-30); no written redistribution terms exist. The generated atlas header records exactly that — `license: informal permission from MEK.txt, no written terms (2026-07-30)` — rather than borrowing MonoMEK's CC0. The raw OTFs are never committed; the bake script reads them from a local, untracked path.

**Consequences:** the glossary's **Glyph atlas** and **Pixel-glyph mode** entries change (MEKMODE, octants, 4×3); `packages/table/DESIGN.md`'s render-tier numbers and its "MEKmode license unverified" open question resolve; [#68](https://github.com/oscabriel/supervillain/issues/68)'s implementation parameters become MEKMODE/octant/4×3; [#50](https://github.com/oscabriel/supervillain/issues/50)'s geometry-comparison question is answered ahead of the harness (its multi-terminal, scrolling, wire-diff, and art-pass checks remain); the demotion ladder's pixel-tier numbers are written against 4×3.

**Revisit triggers:** MEK.txt publishes formal license terms (revalidate redistribution and the header wording against them); tofu reports from real players' terminals (→ the braille rung or a text-tier default, per ADR-0007's existing trigger); MEKMODE's announced expansion ships a changed repertoire or metrics (re-run the bake validation against new hashes).
