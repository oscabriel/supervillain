# Draw Steel license research

## Question

What do MCDM's Draw Steel licensing terms (Draw Steel Creator License / whatever currently governs third-party digital tools) permit and forbid for a product like supervillain? Specifically: shipping a subset of ancestries/classes/kits as static game data, naming "Draw Steel" on a marketing page, charging money for the product, and any attribution/branding requirements. Output: a markdown summary asset with citations to the license text, and a clear "what we may ship in v0" verdict.

## Resolution

The current Creator License permits free or paid third-party works using text, rules, mechanics, names, and stats from its specifically enumerated Draw Steel documents; it states no quantity limit or digital-tool exclusion. Compatibility naming is allowed, but the exact independent-product notice must appear in-product and on promotional pages, and endorsement must not be implied. The compatibility logo is permitted; MCDM/main Draw Steel logos, book art, unlisted-source content, and official status need written permission or separate clearance. SaaS and raw-data resale are not expressly addressed, so counsel should validate the exact paid model. Full findings and quoted primary-source clauses: [Draw Steel license research](../assets/draw-steel-license.md).
