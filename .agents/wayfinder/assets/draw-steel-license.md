# Draw Steel Creator License research

Research checked 2026-07-11. This is a planning summary, not legal advice.

## Primary source and scope

The controlling official page located is MCDM's [DRAW STEEL Creator License](https://www.mcdmproductions.com/draw-steel-creator-license) (canonical short link: <https://mcdm.gg/DS-license>), marked “Last Edit: June 9, 2026.” It says the license applies only to the named documents: _DRAW STEEL Backer Packet #1_, _Draw Steel Backer Packet #2_, _Draw Steel: Heroes_, _Draw Steel: Monsters_, _The Beastheart for Draw Steel_, and _The Summoner_.

The page's FAQ makes that boundary explicit:

> “This license only covers the Documents as stated at the top of the license. Content produced via Patreon, Arcadia, and previous and future products that are not the listed documents are not covered under this license.”

Accordingly, every datum must be traceable to a listed document. “Draw Steel content” is not one undifferentiated licensed corpus.

## Free digital-tool content

The license does not distinguish print works from software or exclude digital tools. Its grant says:

> “If you adhere to these terms, you are allowed to publish works based upon and/or declare compatibility with DRAW STEEL without express permission from MCDM Productions, LLC.”

It expressly permits the relevant kinds of game data:

> “You can reuse and freely reference the DRAW STEEL text, mechanics, and game rules, including proper names, locations, and characters.”

The FAQ further confirms:

> “You can share raw text from the listed products but not screenshots of the pages or artwork.”

Thus a free tool may ship rules text, proper names (including ancestry, class, and kit names), mechanics, and numerical game/stat values taken from the listed documents. The published license states no quantity, percentage, or “small subset” limit. On its face, the permission is not limited to reference snippets. Avoid page screenshots, scans, art, and copied trade dress; the separate art clause says:

> “You agree not to copy or reproduce any art from our books unless you have our explicit permission in writing.”

The FAQ also says there is currently no license coverage for “fonts, border treatments, backgrounds, etc.”

## Naming, attribution, and branding

A marketing page may truthfully say the product is “for Draw Steel” or otherwise declare compatibility, subject to avoiding any appearance of endorsement. The license permits declaring compatibility, but says:

> “You’re not allowed to give the impression that your product is an official DRAW STEEL product or that MCDM endorses or sponsors you or your product in any way.”

The following exact notice is mandatory both on the product's exterior-visible legal text and on its promotional website/storefront:

> “[Product Name] is an independent product published under the DRAW STEEL Creator License and is not affiliated with MCDM Productions, LLC. DRAW STEEL © 2026 MCDM Productions, LLC.”

For a terminal application, “legal text visible on the outside of your product” is not defined. Put the notice in an easily visible in-product legal/about surface as well as the marketing page, and obtain counsel if certainty about placement matters.

Logo treatment is specific:

> “Your product cannot use the MCDM logo or the DRAW STEEL logo unless you have our explicit permission in writing.”

> “You are allowed and encouraged (but not required) to use the DRAW STEEL compatibility logo.”

Therefore use plain-text compatibility wording and optionally the supplied “Powered by Draw Steel” compatibility logo; do not use the MCDM or main DRAW STEEL logos without written permission.

## Charging money

Commercial publication is expressly contemplated. The official introduction says the license “allows anyone to make stuff for DRAW STEEL and either publish it for free or sell it,” and the operative grant does not impose a noncommercial condition, royalty, revenue cap, registration requirement, or content-quantity limit.

Consequently, nothing in the published terms makes a paid hosted product categorically different from a sold content product: either can rely on the license if it complies and uses only covered-document content. A hosting fee does not appear to require special permission merely because Draw Steel data is incidental; directly selling a compliant work based on covered content is likewise allowed. This conclusion is based on the broad grant and absence of a digital-tool or business-model carve-out—not on an explicit clause discussing SaaS, subscriptions, hosting, app stores, data exports, or resale of raw datasets. Those scenarios are not individually addressed, so counsel should review a monetized implementation.

All products also accept the license's indemnity, third-party-IP, governing-law, amendment, and automatic-termination terms. In particular:

> “If you fail to comply with this license, your rights under this License terminate automatically and without notice.”

## What clearly needs written permission or separate clearance

Contact MCDM at the address linked on the license page when the intended product needs:

- MCDM's logo or the main DRAW STEEL logo;
- MCDM book artwork;
- Draw Steel material from Patreon, _Arcadia_, or any other document not in the license's enumerated list;
- fonts, border treatments, backgrounds, or other visual trade dress for which the FAQ says there is currently no license coverage; or
- an official, endorsed, sponsored, or otherwise specially licensed relationship.

The license supplies no special program for “licensed content” bundles and does not say that selling covered text itself requires a bespoke commercial agreement. A direct agreement is therefore a business/risk choice for exclusivity, official status, unlisted content, protected branding/art, or negotiated commercial assurances—not a stated prerequisite to charging for a compliant third-party product.

## Verdict

### What we may ship free at launch

- A static subset—or, under the published text, any quantity—of rules text, ancestry/class/kit names, mechanics, and stat values sourced solely from the documents enumerated by the current license.
- Plain-text “for Draw Steel” compatibility wording, without suggesting endorsement.
- The exact required independent-product/copyright notice both in a conspicuous in-product legal/about surface and on the marketing page/storefront.
- Optionally, the supplied DRAW STEEL compatibility logo.
- No MCDM/main DRAW STEEL logos, art, screenshots/scans, copied layout, or unlisted-source content.

### What needs a commercial MCDM conversation

A conversation and written permission are clearly required for protected MCDM/main DRAW STEEL logos, book art, and content outside the enumerated documents. It is also needed for official/endorsed positioning or any bespoke licensed-content relationship. Charging for a compliant product is itself permitted by the public license and does **not** clearly require that conversation; nevertheless, SaaS and raw-data resale are not expressly analyzed in the license, so launch counsel should validate the exact paid model and notice placement before monetization.
